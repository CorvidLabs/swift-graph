## Requirements

- **REQ-swift-graph-001** (stable): Graph and edge APIs shall preserve directedness, weights, traversal, path, cycle, and topological behavior.
- **REQ-swift-graph-002** (stable): Tree, heap, queue, deque, and circular-buffer APIs shall preserve ordering, capacity, and error invariants.
- **REQ-swift-graph-003** (stable): Trie and Bloom-filter APIs shall preserve prefix, membership, union, intersection, and probabilistic guarantees.
- **REQ-swift-graph-004** (stable): Native verification shall build and run the complete deterministic Swift test suite on normal runners.

## Constraints

- Platform minimums and Swift compatibility remain those declared by the package.

## Out of Scope

- Changing APIs, algorithm complexity, dependencies, releases, or Pages deployment.
